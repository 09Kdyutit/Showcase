# Product

## Register

product

*(Note: the app surfaces — dashboard, portfolio editor, audit, interviews — are product register. Marketing routes `/`, `/pricing`, `/for-career-services` are brand register; treat them as brand when working on them.)*

## Users

Final-year students, new graduates, and 0–2 year professionals in tech and tech-adjacent roles; secondarily career switchers and university career-services teams. They arrive anxious about thin résumés and want to feel prepared and credible without overstating real experience. On the landing page they are skimming on a phone or laptop between applications, deciding in seconds whether this is another generic AI résumé toy or something that will actually help them get hired.

## Product Purpose

Showcase turns one résumé into a connected job-search workspace: AI-generated editable case-study portfolio, Evidence Audit scoring (0–100 across 11 dimensions), role matching with application kits and cover letters, ATS checks, mock-interview practice, and Pro publishing to a live portfolio. Success = a visitor believes "this is the serious tool for proving what I can do" and starts a free portfolio.

## Brand Personality

Confident, kinetic, evidence-first. The interface itself is the proof: a portfolio product must look like the best portfolio the visitor has ever seen. Dark midnight-blue world with saturated electric-blue light as the signature material — glows, beams, and depth, not flatness. Premium-tool energy (the visitor should feel they're getting unfair leverage), never corporate-HR sterile.

## Anti-references

- Minimal/flat/beige SaaS landing pages — the founder explicitly rejects flat, restrained, "safe" design.
- Generic AI-tool templates: identical icon-card grids, tiny uppercase eyebrow labels on every section, hero-metric clichés.
- Anything that reads "résumé builder from 2019" — clip-art job seekers, stock handshake photos, corporate blue-on-white.
- Overclaiming: no fabricated user counts, hiring stats, or testimonials (see `.agents/product-marketing.md` Claim Safety — social proof requires current authoritative evidence).

## Design Principles

1. **Practice what you preach** — a portfolio builder's own page must be portfolio-grade craft; every section battle-tested visually.
2. **Show the product, don't describe it** — real UI, real artifacts (score rings, portfolio frames, application kits) beat abstract illustration.
3. **Light is the material** — the electric-blue-on-midnight glow system carries the brand; commit to it rather than hedging with neutrals.
4. **One narrative, told once** — hero → journey → features → proof → pricing → FAQ → CTA; never re-explain the same story in parallel sections.
5. **Honest confidence** — bold visuals, verifiable claims; every stat and quote on the page must be true or clearly placeholder until it is.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.1 AA target. Body text ≥4.5:1 on the midnight background. Every animation honors `prefers-reduced-motion` with instant/crossfade fallbacks; content is never gated behind scroll-triggered reveals. Interactive targets ≥44px on mobile; sticky mobile CTA preserved.
